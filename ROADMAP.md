# Roadmap

Everything in the [README](README.md) tables exists and is downloadable today.
This file is the other list: things that are planned and not written yet.

They're kept separate on purpose. A repo that lists a tool with no code behind
it is asking you to take its word for something, which is the opposite of what
the rest of this is for.

## Planned

| Tool | What it would do |
|---|---|
| **Pitch Deck Builder** | Builds an investor, partner, or client deck slide by slide with speaker notes, blending 5 narrative frameworks and adapting the mix to who's in the room. Output pastes straight into Gamma or Google Slides. |
| **Marketing Folklore Registry** | A running list of marketing statistics that get repeated everywhere and are provably wrong, plus a checker that flags them in a draft. |
| **Email Deliverability Checklist** | A one-page pre-send checklist covering the technical setup that decides whether a campaign lands in the inbox or in spam. The most boring thing here and probably the most useful. |

No dates. These land when they're done and have earned their place the same way
everything else did, by catching something real in production first.

## Not planned

The weekly drops end Sep 23 2026, and after that this goes to maintenance mode:
bugs and price-table updates keep getting fixed, new features don't get added.
That's stated up front so October's quiet doesn't read as abandonment.

If you want something that isn't here, the license is MIT. Fork it.
